# Judging criteria (equal weight, 10 points each)

Stage One is pass/fail: fits the theme and reasonably uses Kiro.
Insufficient use of Kiro = disqualification. Judging may be done by humans
or by automated AI analysis, and judges may score from video + text alone.

1. **Potential Impact**: real problem, real audience, useful, intuitive, accessible.
2. **Technological Implementation**: depth and variety of Kiro usage
   (specs, hooks, steering, MCP, powers, PBT, custom agents, Crew).
3. **Design**: complete, coherent, polished product experience.
4. **Innovation**: novel concept, unusual technologies combined uniquely.
5. **Presentation**: end-to-end demo, clear problem/audience/why.

## Rules for every change
- Before adding a feature, name the criterion it serves. If none, don't build it.
- Prefer finishing and polishing the MVP over starting stretch goals.
- Keep `docs/JUDGING.md` (criterion to evidence map) and `docs/KIRO_USAGE.md` true and current.
- Accessibility is part of Impact and Design: WCAG AA contrast, keyboard
  navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion`, state never conveyed by color alone.
- No third-party trademarks or logos anywhere (UI, demo data, video).
